========================================================================
BACKTEST HONESTY CHECKLIST
Guardrails that keep results realistic (no fills you couldn't get live)
========================================================================

Enforced centrally in ga_core.py, which drives ALL THREE tools:
  - PyGeneticAlgo.py  (the GA optimizer)
  - OOS Tester.py     (out-of-sample validator)
  - Forward Test.py   (single pinned-strategy forward test)
So every rule below is applied identically everywhere. An in-sample
winner reproduces bar-for-bar out-of-sample and in forward testing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
GOLDEN RULE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
If a fill or a decision depends on information you would NOT have had at
that instant in live trading, it is a cheat. Cut it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE 14 GUARDRAILS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. NO LOOKAHEAD.
   Every signal (entry filter, breakout/dip trigger, custom exit
   condition) reads only COMPLETED bars (i-1, i-2, ...). The current
   bar's close is never used to decide something that happens during
   that same bar.

2. ENTRY PAYS THE SPREAD.
   Breakout entries fill at max(reference, open) + entry spread - you
   lift the ask, never the ideal breakout print. Controlled by
   ENTRY_SPREAD_TICKS (set = 2 to model a post-confirmation stop-limit).
   Dip entries are resting limits and fill at their price.

3. EVERY EXIT PAYS THE 1-TICK SPREAD.
   All exits fill at the bid (minus SPREAD_SLIPPAGE). No mid-market fills.

4. FILLS SNAP TO THE TICK GRID.
   round_to_tick on every entry, exit, and stop/target LEVEL. No sub-tick
   fantasy prices.

5. COMMISSION BOTH SIDES.
   2 x COMMISSION_PER_SIDE per share deducted per round trip.

6. SL PRIORITY ON AMBIGUOUS BARS.
   When a bar's range covers BOTH the stop and the target, assume the
   stop hit first (worst case). Never book the winner on a bar that
   could have stopped you out.

7. SIGNAL EXITS FILL AT THE NEXT OPEN.
   N-bar cap and custom-condition exits decide on bar i and fill at
   open[i+1] minus spread. You can't act on a bar until it closes.

8. NO FREE PASS ON THE ENTRY BAR.
   The ATR stop/target is armed on the entry bar itself, so a same-bar
   stopout is booked. (A loser that stops out on bar 0 must NOT survive
   to the next bar - that was an optimistic bug, now fixed.)

9. NO SAME-BAR RE-ENTRY.
   After any exit, the earliest a new trade can open is the next bar
   (REENTRY_COOLDOWN_BARS). Time only moves forward.

10. GAP-THROUGH FILLS AT THE GAP, NOT THE LEVEL.
    A stop that gaps through fills at min(open, stop) minus spread -
    never better than where the market actually was.

11. TRAILING STOP IS CAUSAL.
    It references the PREVIOUS bar's Close/Low, ratchets up only, and
    never tightens using the current bar's data.

12. SESSION GATE + EOD FLATTEN.
    Entries only inside session hours (SESSION_START..SESSION_END); any
    open position is force-flattened at the session close, minus spread.

13. WARMUP GUARD.
    No trading until indicators (ATR) have enough history. No entries on
    partial / NaN indicator values.

14. ONE ENGINE, ONE TRUTH.
    ga_core.py is the single source of truth. Change a rule here and the
    GA, OOS Tester, and Forward Test all inherit it - no divergence
    between how you optimize and how you validate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE THIS LIST
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before adding any new feature to the engine (new exit type, new entry
model, new filter), walk it against all 14 points. If the change would
let in a fill or decision that violates the golden rule, it is inflating
results - redesign it so it can't.
